## Description

Lugha Desk is free, open-source Windows software for local text translation involving African languages. The complete Store package includes its translation model and native engine. Once installation is complete, translation works offline without a cloud translation service, paid API or separate translation account.

It offers English routes with 19 experimental language options: Swahili, Zulu, Xhosa, Southern Sotho, Tswana, Afrikaans, Somali, Luganda, Kinyarwanda, Amharic, Oromo, Hausa, Igbo, Lingala, Malagasy, Nyanja, Shona, Wolof and Yoruba. Language options are not a claim of independently validated accuracy. Many African languages are not included.

The app uses a local generative AI model to produce translations. Known meaning errors remain, including borrowing/lending confusion and wrong-language output outside the small reference-checked dictionary. Review results before use. Short greeting corrections do not establish reliability for longer text or specialized subjects. Do not rely on unreviewed translations for high-stakes decisions.

Translate up to 600 characters at a time. Loading progress and cancellation are available. Translation text is not automatically uploaded, and the app does not automatically save translation history. Users can voluntarily report incorrect or inappropriate output through the support contact shown in the app; only share information you choose to send.
